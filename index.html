    📖 Which datum / grid do I need?
    Phone or Google Maps? → WGS84 (default). Your GPS already gives WGS84 — just paste it.

    Reading an old L7010 paper sheet? → Kertau 1948. Pick Kertau, type the lat/lng printed on the sheet. The two datums differ by ~150–200 m.

    Grids: “6-digit” = 100 m square (±50 m); “8-digit” = 10 m square (±5 m). Typing a short grid jumps to the square centre.

    Coverage: Peninsular Malaysia — E 176,138–734,057 · N 124,944–744,338 (≈ 1.13–6.72°N, 99.59–104.6°E). Outside = extrapolated, flagged.

    Math: +proj=omerc +no_uoff +lat_0=4 +lonc=102.25 +alpha=323.0257905 +gamma=323.13010236111 +k=0.99984 +x_0=804670.24 +y_0=0 +ellps=evrst69 +towgs84=-11,851,5 (EPSG:3168 via Proj4js).
